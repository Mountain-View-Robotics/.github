SJ Tech — Practical Robotics for Real-World Deployment

Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Nanjing, China, SJ Tech is committed to developing cost-effective dexterous robotic hands and intelligent automation systems that prioritize real-world integration over showroom complexity.

Our product families range from modular dexterous hands (F6, F11, S7, F4) to mobile manipulation robots combining arms, 3D vision, and navigation, as well as end-to-end smart pharmacy automation systems capable of processing over 350 prescriptions per hour and 1,000 boxes per hour for replenishment.

We serve retail pharmacy, semiconductor inspection, and smart service robotics, with a replace-first warranty policy that enables flexible deployment and easy maintenance. Our goal is to help customers turn robotic tasks into practical, reliable workflows—quickly and affordably.
